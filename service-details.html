Balayage & Ombré

Hand-painted color that looks sun-lit, grows out gracefully and suits almost every base shade.

Color that moves with you

Balayage means “to sweep” in French. Your colorist paints lightener freehand, so brightness sits where light would naturally catch your hair.

Unlike foil highlights, there are no harsh start lines. Ombré takes the same technique deeper, fading from your natural root into a lighter end. Most guests choose balayage for a soft result and ombré for a bolder one.

What the treatment involves

We begin with a consultation and a strand test when needed. Lightener is applied by hand in sweeping strokes, processed under close watch, then toned to your target shade and sealed with a bond-repairing treatment.

Finish includes a gloss, a blow-dry and a take-home care plan. Ombré adds a soft root blend for a graduated finish.

Benefits

  • Soft regrowthNo hard line, so you can wait months between visits.
  • Made for your faceBrightness is placed to frame and flatter, not applied by formula.
  • Gentler on hairBond protection runs through the whole service.
  • Easy to styleColor adds movement even when your hair is air-dried.

The treatment, step by step

  1. Consultation

    We review your history, goals and lifestyle, and plan.

  2. Painting

    Lightener is hand-painted where you want brightness.

  3. Toning

    Once lifted, we tone and gloss to the agreed shade.

  4. Finish

    Bond treatment, blow-dry and a home-care plan.

Recommended hair types

Balayage works across lengths and textures. Tell us if your hair is heavily colored or damaged, as we may suggest a repair treatment first.

  • Straight
  • Wavy
  • Curly
  • Coily (with a curl specialist)
  • Medium to long lengths
  • Dark to light-brown bases
  • Previously colored (consultation)
