The 2026 guide to choosing a haircut that suits you

By Amara VossNov 4, 20267 min readChoosing the Right Haircut

In this article

  1. Start with texture, not trends
  2. Be honest about your routine
  3. What to say in a consultation
  4. Plan the months after

Choosing a haircut should feel exciting, not stressful. Here is how our stylists help guests decide, step by step.

Start with texture, not trends

Before you save a photo, look at your own hair. Fine straight hair, thick waves and tight curls each hold a cut differently, so the same style can look completely different on you.

Be honest about your routine

If you have five minutes in the morning, ask for a cut that dries well on its own. A great haircut should work with your habits instead of demanding new ones.

The best haircut is the one you can recreate on a normal Tuesday morning.

What to say in a consultation

Bring two or three photos, plus one photo of what you do not want. Describe how you wear your hair most days, and mention any past color or heat damage.

Plan the months after

Ask how the cut will grow out, how often you will need a trim and which products will help. A stylist who answers clearly is a stylist worth keeping.

Amara Voss

Creative director at ÉLAN. Fifteen years of cutting, color and editorial work.
