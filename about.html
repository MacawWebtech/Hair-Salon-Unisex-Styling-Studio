About ÉLAN

A small studio with a long memory for faces, hair and the way you like it done.

Our salon story

ÉLAN started with one question: what if a salon visit never felt rushed?

Amara Voss opened the studio in 2014 after years of working fast-paced editorial and high-volume salons. She wanted time to consult properly, cut precisely and send people home with hair they could style themselves.

Today our team of fourteen shares that approach. We keep our schedule deliberately loose, so every guest gets the time their hair needs.

Philosophy and mission

Listen first

Our mission is to translate what you describe into a style that suits your hair, face and routine.

Protect the hair

We choose gentler techniques and bond-protecting products so color and cuts leave hair stronger.

Teach as we go

We show you how to recreate your style at home, so the result lasts well beyond the chair.

Our approach to beauty and hairstyling

Every appointment follows the same rhythm: consult, cut or color, then coach. We assess your hair’s history, texture and density before planning anything, and we are honest when a goal needs more than one visit.

Fashion informs our work, but your lifestyle leads it. A great cut should work on a Tuesday morning as well as on a night out.

Atmosphere and experience

Soft light, warm wood and low music. The studio is built to feel more like a private living room than a salon floor.

Expect tea or espresso on arrival, a scalp massage at every wash and a few quiet minutes with your stylist before the scissors come out.
