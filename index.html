About ÉLAN

A quieter kind of salon

We opened ÉLAN to slow the appointment down. Longer slots, a proper consultation and stylists who finish what they start.

Our team trains in classic cutting and modern color, and we keep our client list small enough to remember your hair history. Whether you want a ten-minute trim or a full change, you will leave with a plan for the months after, too.
